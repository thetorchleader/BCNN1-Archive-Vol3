2016 WHCD Host Larry Wilmore Teases President Obama Roast

April 26, 2016 Media

The choppa is about to fly. (Photo: Stephen Lovekin, Getty Images for Comedy Central)

Saturday will, admittedly, be a “scary night” for comedian Larry Wilmore. But as host of this year’s White House Correspondents’ Dinner, the comedian-in-chief promises he won’t hold back.

In an interview with CBS This Morning Monday, the comedian shared that, while 90% of the night is CBS jokes, he does plan to get in a few jabs at President Obama. “Well, I like Obama a lot, but remember, I always supported him because he was black. I can attack his positions all I want,” he joked.

Wilmore is also expected to tackle racial issues and potentially skewer the 2016 presidential race — or, as he so eloquently describes it on The Nightly Show with Larry Wilmore, “the Unblackening of the White House.” His routine will likely follow in the vein of his late-night program, which unabashedly tackles racial unrest.

Wilmore previously told CBS News’ Jim Axelrod that The Nightly Show homed in on racial issues because he and his staff felt they were the best equipped to helm the conversation. “The thing that we felt we had the permission to talk about more than anybody when we started was all the racial problems that were going on in the country,” he said in an interview Sunday. “In fact we joked when we first came on the air that, ‘Ugh, all the ‘good’ bad racial stuff has already happened. There’s nothing left to cover.’ It’s like, ‘Oh, surprise! Something happened!’ And people always say, ‘Well, why you gotta talk about race?’ And bah, bah, bah. Well, why not? I mean, it is one of the biggest issues of our day and of our times.”

SOURCE: Jaleesa M. Jones, USA TODAY Entertain This!
