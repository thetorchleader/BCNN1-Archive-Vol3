12 Normal Habits That May Be Messing Up Your Life

May 2, 2016 Lifestyle

STOP: JUMPING OUT OF BED AND INTO THE SHOWER
Before getting out of bed tomorrow morning do this—strike a pose. By assuming a pose of strength, like the ‘V for Victory’ position with arms up, for just two minutes, ‘a person can instantly become more powerful,’ says Harvard psychologist Amy Cuddy in her book, Presence: Bringing Your Boldest Self to Your Biggest Challenges. ‘Let your body tell you you’re powerful and deserving, and you become more present, enthusiastic and authentically yourself.’ In other words, ‘Fake it till you become it.’ Here’s how to be the most interesting person in the room.

STOP: THROWING AWAY BANANAS WITH BROWN SPOTS
‘Eww, gross! My banana’s brown.’ Are you kidding? When bananas go brown, that’s when they’re at their best. They’re sweeter, tastier, and pack loads of health benefits. ‘A brown banana has a higher level of antioxidants than yellow or green, unripe bananas,’ according to Livestrong.com. ‘It’s also easier to digest for people with digestive ailments, including irritable bowel and functional abdominal bloating.’

STOP: MAKING LISTS
The first item on your to-do list should be: Throw out this list. ‘To-do lists are where important tasks go to die,’ Kevin Kruse, a time management expert, told the Daily Mail. That’s because we only do around 40 percent of what’s on the list. The problem: the tasks are not generally prioritized, and trying to tick them off leads to anxiety. Instead, says Kruse, block off 15 to 30 minute increments during the day to concentrate on your most important projects.

STOP: BEING A PRUDE
Yes, your steely smile at the jerk who cut the line at the supermarket was very genteel of you. But you should have responded with a string of invectives. The potty mouths at Psychology Today tell us that cussing has loads of benefits. ‘By swearing we show, if only to ourselves, that we are not passive victims but empowered to react and fight back,’ according to Neel Burton, MD. Cursing is also an effective pain reliever. A British study found that people who swear are able to hold their hands in ice-water for twice as long as people who don’t curse. So next time that woman cuts the line, tell her where to stick her yellow, less-tasty bananas. Here are other times it’s perfectly OK to curse.

STOP: WASTING THE GOOD VODKA ON THE APPLETINIS!
Okay, put the Grey Goose down. You heard me. Now, grab the bottle that you found way, way, down on the bottom shelf of the liquor store. That’s good enough for the fruity cocktail you’re making. After all, the vodka will be masked by all the other flavors. The good stuff, says Slate.com, should ‘only be mixed with ice and/or tonic water and are best drunk straight and straight from the freezer.’

STOP: READING SO MUCH
And watch TV! A British poll found that those with a television in their bedroom have sex twice as much as those who don’t, and two-thirds of those people credit TV, giving ‘the boob tube’ a whole new meaning. A TV in the boudoir, said those polled, led to watching erotic programs together or having sex while watching their favorite shows. ‘Those who don’t have a TV in their bedroom use their bedroom mainly for the purpose of sleeping,’ a spokesman for the pollsters told the Daily Mail. Imagine that.
