12-Year-Old Cousin of Slain High School Football Star Zaevion Dobson Killed in Drive-by Shooting After Anti-Violence Basketball Game

April 19, 2016 Black, National

The 12-year-old cousin of a rising Tennessee high school football star shot dead in December was killed Saturday night, police said.

Jajuan Hubert Latham was shot in the head in Knoxville just hours after an event that honored his slain cousin, Zaevion Dobson, NBC station WBIR reported.

Zaevion, 15, died while shielding three friends from a hail of gunfire; President Barack Obama used his death as a platform to call for tighter gun laws, tweeting: “Zaevion Dobson died saving three friends from getting shot. He was a hero at 15. What’s our excuse for not acting?”

Investigators believe Jajuan was killed Saturday night while sitting in the back seat of his father’s SUV, which was at a park in Knoxville. Gunfire erupted between two vehicles that were driving by the park, and a large group gathered there, police said in a statement.
