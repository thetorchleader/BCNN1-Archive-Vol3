3 Terror Suspects Arrested in Brussels Raids

March 25, 2016 World

The arrest of three people Friday in raids in several districts of Brussels is linked to a French operation Thursday night that purportedly disrupted a terror plot in an advanced stage, Belgian prosecutors said.

One man carrying a suitcase with bomb materials was shot in the leg and arrested in Schaerbeek, the same Brussels district where police earlier this week uncovered a bomb-making factory used in Tuesday’s terror attacks that killed 31 people, Belgian state television reported.

The federal prosecutor’s office says a total of three people were arrested in a series of raids in Schaerbeek, Forest and Saint-Gilles. Three of the terrorists in Tuesday’s attacks had been living in a Schaerbeek apartment where police later found a bomb-making factory and flags of the Islamic State extremist group.

The latest arrests are believed linked to the arrest in Argenteuil, France, on Thursday of Reda Kriket, who was convicted in absentia of terrorist activities last year along with the suspected ringleader of the deadly Nov. 13 attacks on Paris, the Associated Press reported.

French Interior Minister Bernard Cazeneuve confirmed the arrest of a French national who appeared to be at an advanced stage of planning a terrorist attack. He said the suspect did not appear to be linked directly to the Brussels attacks but belonged to a terrorist network and may have some connections to Paris attacks ringleader Abdelhamid Abaaoud.

“He belongs to a terrorist network that sought to strike our country,” Cazeneuve said, without confirming the identity of the suspect.

Friday’s raids are part of an operation that began Thursday night in the Jette and Schaerbeek neighborhoods that netted six arrests of people allegedly connected with Tuesday’s attacks, according to Belgian federal prosecutors.
