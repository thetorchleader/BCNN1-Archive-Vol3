2 Dead In Washington, D. C. Suburb Shootings; Suspect Arrested

May 6, 2016 National

Police take Eulalio Tordil, 62, a suspect in three fatal shootings in the Washington, D.C., area into custody in Bethesda, Md., on May 6, 2016.
(Photo: Alex Brandon, AP)

Police in a Washington, D.C. suburb arrested a federal law enforcement officer wanted for killing his estranged wife and said they believe he was also involved in two fatal shootings Friday at a grocery store and a nearby shopping mall.

Eulalio Tordil, 62, an officer with the Federal Protective Service, was taken into custody at a strip mall in Montgomery County, Md., near the site of Friday’s shootings.

He was already being sought for allegedly killing his estranged wife, Gladys Tordil, 44, on Thursday in the parking lot of High Point High School in Beltsville, Md.

Darryl McSwain, assistant police chief of Montgomery County, said Tordil was arrested without incident by a tactical squad. He said there was “reason to believe” Tordil was involved in Friday’s shooting incidents that took place 45 minutes and only miles apart.

The first shooting erupted at the Westfield Montgomery Mall in Bethesda, Md., where three people were shot, one fatally. Less than an hour later, a woman was shot and killed eight miles away at a Giant grocery store in the Aspen Hill area of Montgomery County, also in Maryland.

Police said the man killed in the mall shooting spree was apparently targeted by the gunman, while the two people injured were shot trying to come to his aid. One of the two bystanders was in grave condition and the other, a woman, suffered non-life-threatening injuries.

Westfield Montgomery Mall, located in a tony area northwest of the District of Columbia, was placed on lockdown along with nearby Suburban Hospital.
