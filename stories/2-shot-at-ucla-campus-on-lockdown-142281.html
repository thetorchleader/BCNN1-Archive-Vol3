2 Shot at UCLA, Campus on Lockdown

June 1, 2016 National

In this image made from video, police respond to a report of a possible shooter at the University of California, Los Angeles, Wednesday, June 1, 2016, in Los Angeles.
(Photo: KABC-7 via AP)

Two people were shot at the University of California Los Angeles on Wednesday, and administrators placed the school on lockdown as police searched for a possible shooter.

The conditions of the two victims were not immediately known, campus spokesman Ricardo Vazquez said. The university asked students and faculty to shelter in place. ATF tweeted it was sending agents to the scene.

Police were investigating the scene of the shooting at the Engineering IV building, also known as Boelter Hall. Aerial television coverage showed groups of heavily armed police running up stairs on the south side of the building. Ambulances and fire crews arrived as a precaution.

Although other colleges already recessed classes for summer, UCLA’s quarter is still underway.
