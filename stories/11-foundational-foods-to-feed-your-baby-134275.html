11 Foundational Foods to Feed Your Baby

April 16, 2016 Health

Good nutrition is essential for healthy kids. But parents today are bombarded with confusing, and sometimes even harmful information. Nutritional guidelines are constantly changing, and parents don’t know who to trust for medically sound, proven advice that works.

Dr. Tanya Altmann is a practicing pediatrician and spokesperson for the American Academy of Pediatrics as well as a mother of three young boys. She cuts through the noise and offers expert advice with a simple program that follows the safest, best practices for feeding babies and young children.

Watch her discuss the 11 foundational foods that critical to brain development and growth, as well as how to manage your child’s weight and which beverages are best for your little one.
