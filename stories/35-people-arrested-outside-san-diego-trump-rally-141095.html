35 People Arrested Outside San Diego Trump Rally

May 28, 2016 National

REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst

At least 35 people were arrested outside a Donald Trump campaign rally in San Diego on Friday. Demonstrators jumped over crowd control barriers, continuing a series of confrontations between protesters and police at Trump’s rallies in New Mexico and California this week.
