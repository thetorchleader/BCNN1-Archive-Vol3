17-Year-Old Daughter of Johnny Depp Defends Him on Instagram

May 30, 2016 Entertainment

Lily-Rose Depp, right, called her father “the sweetest most loving person I know.” (Photo: Wire Image; AP)

Lily-Rose Depp, Johnny Depp’s 17-year-old daughter, has defended her dad on Instagram without explicitly saying he is innocent. 

Since Amber Heard was granted a restraining order against Johnny Depp, Lily-Rose posted a screengrab Sunday of a news story that said: “There was no evidence of any crime” and “The person reporting the crime [Heard] did not insist on a report…”

A photo posted by Lily-Rose Depp (@lilyrose_depp) on May 29, 2016 at 2:50pm PDT

//platform.instagram.com/en_US/embeds.js
Before that, Lily-Rose posted a throwback photo, presumably of herself and her father, with this caption: “My dad is the sweetest most loving person I know…”

 

My dad is the sweetest most loving person I know, he’s been nothing but a wonderful father to my little brother and I, and everyone who knows him would say the same ❤️

A photo posted by Lily-Rose Depp (@lilyrose_depp) on May 29, 2016 at 4:57am PDT

 

According to TMZ, Lily-Rose’s mother and Johnny’s former partner, Vanessa Paradis, also showed her support for Johnny, in a letter.

“Johnny Depp… is a sensitive, loving and loved person, and I believe in all my heart that these recent allegations being made are outrageous,” she wrote. “In all the years I have known Johnny, he has never been physically abusive with me…”
